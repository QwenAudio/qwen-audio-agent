# Get Started in 3 Minutes: Talk to Your AI, Hands-Free

> Written for people who have never touched a terminal. One Mac, three steps.

## What is it?

**Qwen Audio Agent** is a small glowing orb that lives on your Mac desktop. You talk to it; it understands, gets things done, and answers out loud — no typing involved.

Two things make it different from ordinary voice assistants:

- **Interrupt it anytime**, like a real conversation — it follows your new thought instead of getting confused
- **Heavy work runs in the background** while you keep chatting; when it finishes, it simply tells you "all done"

## Step 1 — Install

1. Download the `mac-universal.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/QwenAudio/qwen-audio-agent/releases/latest)
2. Open it and drag **Qwen Audio Agent** into Applications
3. Launch it from Launchpad

> If macOS warns about an unidentified developer: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".

## Step 2 — First-run setup

The app walks you through a short wizard:

1. **A voice service key (DashScope)** — the app's "ears and mouth". Create a free Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian) account and generate the key in its console. The key stays on your own computer.
2. **Pick a workhorse agent** — if you already use Claude Code or Codex, hook them up. **Don't have any? Choose front-end-only mode** — you still get a fully realtime voice assistant, and you can attach agents later.

## Step 3 — Start talking

Say things like:

- "Remind me to check the oven in twenty minutes"
- "Give me a short explanation of black holes"
- "Give me three ideas for tonight's dinner"

Talk while it talks. Interrupt anytime. Change your mind mid-sentence.

## Daily-life tips

- **It hides itself** when idle; or just say "你可以退下了" ("you may go")
- **Bring it back** with `⇧⌘ Space` or the menu-bar icon
- **Wake word**: enable it in settings, then say "你好千问" — detection runs fully on-device, audio never leaves your Mac
- **Two looks**: pick between the fluid audio orb and the liquid gradient orb in settings

## Prefer typing?

The same app ships a web interface — same conversations, no voice required.
